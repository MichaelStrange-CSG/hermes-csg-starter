# Set up Hermes on your computer

Time: about **45-60 minutes**. Your AI does almost all of it. You'll paste one line, sign in once, click
Allow a few times, and answer some questions about how you work.

You need:
- A Mac or a Windows PC (Windows 10 version 1809 or newer, or Windows 11).
- Your own paid Claude account (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise). Hermes runs on it.
- About 10 GB of free disk space.

---

## Step 1: Open an AI coding tool

Use the Claude desktop app if you don't already use another one:

1. Install it from **claude.com/download** and sign in with **your own** Claude account.
2. Click **Code** at the top. Under the message box choose **Local**, then pick any folder when it asks
   (your Documents folder is fine). If it asks whether you trust the folder, choose **Yes**.

Codex, Cursor and similar tools work too, as long as they can run commands on your computer.

## Step 2: Paste this and send it

> Install Hermes from https://github.com/MichaelStrange-CSG/hermes-csg-starter

## Step 3: Help when it asks

- **Allow prompts:** the AI asks before it runs each install step. Choose **Yes** (or "always allow").
- **Claude sign-in:** a browser window may open asking you to sign in to Claude. Sign in with your own
  account and click **Authorize**.
- **Mac only:** a window may ask to install "Command Line Tools". Click **Install**, enter your Mac
  password if asked, and tell the AI when it's done.
- **Waiting:** two steps take 5-20 minutes each. That's normal; leave the app open and the computer awake.
- **Personality interview:** at the end, the AI asks how you work and what you want from your assistant,
  and suggests starting text. Change anything you like. This is what makes Hermes yours, so take your time.

## Step 4: Start using Hermes

- **Mac:** open **Hermes** from Launchpad or Spotlight (⌘ Space, type "Hermes").
- **Windows:** open **Hermes** from the Start menu or the Desktop shortcut.

You have two profiles: **Work** (opens by default) and **home**. Each has its own memory, so keep work
in Work and personal things in home.

## Step 5: Send the report if needed

If either of these is true, send the file **Hermes-Install-Report.md** from your Desktop to the person who
shared this link with you:
- you have a **Windows** PC or an **Intel Mac**, or
- anything failed.

It has no passwords or personal details, only your computer type and the check results.

---

### Good to know
- Hermes runs on **your** Claude plan and shares its usage limits with your other Claude use.
- **Don't run `hermes update`.** This setup is pinned to a tested version; ask before changing versions.
- To tune Hermes' personality later, tell Hermes: *"Read SOUL-BUILDER.md and tune my SOUL.md."*
- If something goes wrong partway through, close nothing. Tell the AI what you see, or send the report.
- If you stopped partway, paste the same line again later. It picks up where it left off.
